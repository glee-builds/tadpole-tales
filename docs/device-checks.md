# Device checks

A computer test can't prove the app works on a real phone or on the iPad mini (5th generation). Run this list on **both** at the end of each phase, and fill in the table at the bottom. It takes about 30 minutes. If a step doesn't do what it says, write down what happened in the notes column and carry on.

You need: the phone, the iPad, and Safari (the blue compass app) on each.

Two words used below: the **Home Screen** is where your app icons live, and a **backup** is one file holding every book, which you save somewhere safe.

## 1. Write down the version

On each device: **Settings → General → About**. Look at **iOS Version** (phone) or **iPadOS Version** (iPad). Write it at the top of the table.

## 2. Install

1. Open Safari and go to https://glee-builds.github.io/tadpole-tales/. You should see the bookshelf.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up), scroll the list, and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. A tadpole icon appears on the Home Screen.
3. Close Safari. Tap the new icon. The app opens full screen with no Safari bar at the top.
4. Swipe up from the bottom to close the app. Turn on **Airplane Mode** (Settings, top switch). This cuts off the internet.
5. Tap the icon again. You should see the bookshelf, and any books you made earlier. Leave Airplane Mode on for step 3 below, then turn it off.

## 3. The grown-up page and its device check

The device check asks this device what it can really do.

1. On the bookshelf, press and hold **For grown-ups** for about 2 seconds. The grown-up page opens.
2. Find **Device check**. Tap each button in turn: Save and reread, Keep storage, Home Screen, Read aloud, Record 3 seconds, Play it back, Save a file, Browser. Sound and the microphone only start from a tap. If asked to allow the microphone, tap **Allow**.
3. Each line shows a tick (worked) or a cross (didn't) and a short reason. Tap **Copy results**.
4. Copy each tick or cross into the table at the end, with a note for any cross.

## 4. The lesson card and the example

1. On the bookshelf, tap **+ Make a new book**. A lesson card opens: **Lesson 1** at the top, a lesson title, one sentence about the idea, and two lines marked **✏️ Before** and **✨ After**. Check the words are easy to read on this device.
2. Tap **👀 Look at an example**. The example story opens, laid out like a finished book.
3. Tap **🔍 Find it** at the top. A question about the idea appears. Tap a page (or a sentence, for some lessons). The place where the idea is lights up with **👀 Here**, and the top-right button changes to **✓ Done**. Nothing is marked right or wrong. To read the story again, tap **‹ Story** at the top left.
4. Tap **✓ Done** (or **✕ Skip**) to go back to the lesson card. Tap **←** at the top left to go back again.
5. Once a book is open, the top of its page screen shows **📖** and a short reminder of the lesson. Tap it to open the card again.
6. On the bookshelf, each lesson you have opened is a card under **🐸 Tadpole's books**, with **📖 Read it** (the example story) and **💡 Lesson** (the card).

## 5. Plan your story and the Ideas panel

1. On the lesson card, tap **✏️ Try it in your book**. The screen says **Plan your story** and asks the first of five questions, such as "Who is in your story?". Under it, "Like: ..." gives an example.
2. Type an answer on the app's own keyboard. The button reads **Skip ›** while the box is empty, and **Next ›** once something is there. On the last question it reads **✏️ Start writing**.
3. To leave planning early, tap **Skip planning** at the top. The writing area opens.
4. On a page of the book, tap **💡 Ideas**. The answers show as small cards. Tap **💡 Ideas** again to hide them.

## 6. After all 8 lessons: pick a lesson

This needs eight finished books, so a grown-up may leave it until last.

1. Tap **+ Make a new book**. The screen **Your new book** asks you to pick one lesson for the book. Each lesson you have opened is a button with its title, and the last button is **🚫 No lesson this time**.
2. Tap a lesson: its card opens. Or tap **🚫 No lesson this time**: planning opens straight away.
3. Tap **←** at the top left to go back to the bookshelf.

## 7. Typing

1. Tap **+ Make a new book**, then **✏️ Try it in your book**, then **Skip planning**. You should see the writing area and the app's own keyboard. The device's usual keyboard should never appear.
2. Type a whole sentence as fast as you can with both thumbs. Every letter should appear, in order, with none missing or doubled.
3. Tap in the middle of a word. The cursor (the blinking line) should jump to where you tapped. Type a letter: it appears there.
4. Press and hold the backspace key. Letters should disappear one after another, faster the longer you hold. Let go and it stops.
5. Does tapping anywhere ever zoom the page or bring up a copy-and-paste bubble? It should not.

## 8. Saving

1. Type a sentence and stop in the middle of it. Don't tap anything else.
2. Right away, swipe up from the bottom and flick the app away to close it.
3. Open the app again from its icon and open the same book. The words you typed should be there.
4. Back on the bookshelf, tap **↻ Refresh** at the bottom. The app reloads and your book is still there.

## 9. Backup and restore

Do this with a book or two on the **phone**, and none (or different ones) on the iPad.

1. On the phone, open the grown-up page and tap **Back up all books**. Save the file somewhere you can reach from the iPad, such as **Files** or iCloud Drive. You should see "Backup made".
2. On the iPad, open the app, press and hold **For grown-ups**, and tap **Restore from a file…**. Pick the file.
3. Go back to the bookshelf. The phone's books should now be on the iPad.
4. If the iPad already had books, check that none of them changed or went missing. Restoring only adds books.
5. Restore the same file a second time. No book should appear twice.

## 10. Stickers (from Phase 2)

Open a book and go to its picture.

1. Tap a sticker in the tray. It appears on the picture.
2. Drag it with one finger. It follows your finger and stays inside the picture.
3. Move it with two taps: tap the sticker, then tap a new spot.
4. Tap a sticker, then tap **Bigger** twice, **Smaller** once, and **Remove**. Each does what it says.
5. Add stickers until the app says the picture is full (12).

## 11. Read to me (from Phase 2)

1. Open a finished book to read it and tap **Read to me**.
2. You should hear the words. The sentence being read is underlined. The word being said is underlined too, but only if the device sends word timing; if it doesn't, the sentence is the only thing that moves. Note which one you saw in the results table.
3. Tap **Stop**. The voice stops and the underline goes away.
4. Turn the volume up and make sure the silent switch is off if you hear nothing.

## 12. Finishing a book (from Phase 2)

Open a book you wrote in and tap **✓ Finish** at the top right.

1. The first question is "What's your book called?". Type a title. The button reads **Skip** while the box is empty, and **Next ›** once something is there.
2. Tap **Next ›**. The next question is "What's your author name?". It is asked only the first time: once a name is saved, this step is left out. Type a name, or tap **Skip**.
3. Next is "Add stickers to your cover". Tap stickers from the tray, or tap **Skip** to leave the cover plain.
4. Tap **Next ›** (or **Skip**). The steps after the cover are in sections 13 to 15.

## 13. After Finish: hear your book, the self-check, make it better

1. **Hear your book.** Tap **🔊 Read to me**. The book is read from page 1. Tap **⏹ Stop** to end it early, then **Next ›**. If the device has no English voice, this step is left out.
2. **The self-check.** The heading asks one thing, such as "Tap the page where your story begins." Some lessons use pages: tap a page tile (its label says **Page** and a number). Those that ask for a second page change the heading to a second question. Other lessons use sentences: tap **Next ›** or **‹ Previous** to change page, then tap the sentence. Once the tap is made, the step moves on by itself. Nothing is marked right or wrong.
3. **"I don't have one yet".** On the self-check, tap **✋ I don't have one yet** at the bottom. A kind card says "That's okay! Want to go back and add it?" Tap **Carry on ›** to go on, or **✏️ Go back and add it** to go back to the book's words.
4. **Make it better.** A card shows one job, such as "Add one more thing that happens in the middle." Tap **✏️ Do it** to go back and write it, or **Not now ›** to go on.

## 14. Spelling check (after "Make it better")

Steps (phone, then iPad): finish a book that has a few misspelled lowercase words, going through the other steps.

1. A **Spelling check** heading shows one word in its sentence, highlighted, with "Did you mean ...?" and a count such as "1 of 3".
2. Tap the suggestion (**✓** and the word): only that word changes in the book. Tap **✋ Keep mine**: nothing changes.
3. Nothing is red or says "wrong"; both buttons are easy to tap; no scrolling.
4. Tap **Skip** (top right) part-way: earlier choices stay, and the kind message shows.
5. Close the app part-way and open it again: chosen words are kept.
6. A book with no misspellings, and a character's name from "Plan your story", are not asked about.
7. In Airplane Mode the step still works (word lists saved offline).

## 15. The kind message (last step)

1. The heading shows the book's title. A card gives a kind line about the page your self-check tapped (or "You finished your book. You're an author!"), with "Read it to someone!" under it.
2. Tap **✓ Done**. The shelf shows "🎉 Your book is on the shelf!", and the book is marked **✓ Finished**.

## 16. Moving a page (from Phase 2)

Open a book with at least three pages.

1. Tap **🖼 Picture** at the top.
2. On page 2, tap **Later ▶**. The page moves to the next place, and its number at the top changes to match. Its words and stickers move with it.
3. Tap **◀ Earlier** to move it back. On the first page **◀ Earlier** can't be tapped; on the last page, **Later ▶** can't be.
4. Tap **✏️ Write** at the top to go back to the words.

## 17. Reading stops (from Phase 2)

Open a finished book and tap **🔊 Read to me** so it starts reading.

1. Tap **Next ›**. Reading stops, the button reads **🔊 Read to me** again, and the next page is shown.
2. Tap **🔊 Read to me** again and let it read a sentence or two. Then tap **✕ Close**. You are back on the bookshelf and no voice is heard.
3. Open the book, tap **🔊 Read to me**, then swipe up from the bottom to close the app. Open the app again from its icon. The book is not reading out loud.

## 18. Stickers after backup and restore (from Phase 2)

Use a book with stickers on a page, that the iPad does not have yet.

1. On the phone, open that book, tap **🖼 Picture**, and note one sticker and where it sits (for example, top left).
2. On the phone, open the grown-up page and tap **Back up all books**. Save the file somewhere both devices can reach.
3. On the iPad, press and hold **For grown-ups**, tap **Restore from a file…**, and pick the file. Open the same book.
4. The same sticker should sit in the same place, at the same size, on the same page, on both devices.

## 19. Tell it (from Phase 4)

Tell it records the writer telling a page out loud. It is off until a grown-up turns it on. Do this on **both** devices; the old iPad mini matters most.

1. On the bookshelf, press and hold **For grown-ups**. Under **Tell it**, tap **Tell it (record your voice): Off**. It changes to **On**. Tap **←** to go back.
2. Open a book. Above **‹ Prev** you should see **🎙 Tell it**, and no **▶ Hear it** yet.
3. Tap **🎙 Tell it**. The first time, a question asks to use the microphone: tap **Allow**. You should see **■ Stop** and a red **● Recording**. Say a sentence or two.
4. Tap **■ Stop**. **● Recording** goes away and **▶ Hear it** appears. Check the microphone is off: on an iPhone, the orange or red microphone dot at the top of the screen goes away within a few seconds.
5. Tap **▶ Hear it**. You should hear what you said, from the loudspeaker at normal loudness (not quietly, as if from the earpiece). Tap **■ Stop** while it plays: it stops.
6. Tap **🎙 Tell it** again, say something different, and tap **■ Stop**. **▶ Hear it** now plays only the new recording.
7. Tap **🎙 Tell it** and say nothing. Wait for about 2 minutes, without touching the screen. The recording stops by itself, with no countdown, and **▶ Hear it** appears.
8. Tap **🎙 Tell it**, then tap **Next ›** while recording. The microphone dot goes away. Tap **‹ Prev**: **▶ Hear it** plays what you said before leaving.
9. Tap **🎙 Tell it**, then swipe up to the Home Screen while recording. The microphone dot goes away. Open the app again: the page is still there, and nothing is recording.
10. Leave the book (tap **←**) and open it again. **▶ Hear it** is still there and still plays.
11. On the grown-up page, tap **Back up all books** and open the file in **Files**: it is a short text file. It holds the books' words, not the recordings.
12. To see what a refusal looks like (optional): in **Settings → Safari → Microphone**, choose **Deny**, then tap **🎙 Tell it**. A short note says a grown-up can allow the microphone; typing still works. Set it back to **Ask** afterwards.
